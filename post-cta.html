Crafting Effective Calls to Action

What is a Call to Action (CTA)?

A Call to Action is a prompt that encourages your peers to take a specific action. In WGU Connect posts, it transforms passive readers into active participants.

Why CTAs Matter

  • ● Boost Engagement: CTAs get more responses and participation
  • ● Build Community: Inviting responses creates dialogue
  • ● Drive Results: Clear direction achieves your goals

Elements of a Strong CTA

Be Specific: Tell peers exactly what to do
Create Value: Explain what's in it for them
Make it Easy: Lower the barrier to participate
Add Urgency: Give a reason to act now
